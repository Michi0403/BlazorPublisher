Behavior contracts added or strengthened
-----------------------------------------
- A committed point/range is owned by its selected clip and is not overwritten by playhead,
  metadata, renderer, layer, or clip-switch synchronization.
- Saved cut sections are a separate many-item collection and do not replace the current
  selection unless explicitly restored.
- Frame regions are normalized source-coordinate objects owned by the selected video layer.
- Video layers and filters deep-clone and persist through load/save, split, duplicate, merge,
  Media Studio result application, and old single-polygon migration.
- The shared canvas renderer is used by Video Studio, publication/Mainframe video preview,
  and visual live-source inputs.
- Mainframe live-source sliders/color edits refresh the canonical Live input controls layer in
  the same edit.
- Visual streaming inputs expose authored effect layers and the complete shared filter set,
  including independently editable chroma key, vignette, grain, and color wash.

-------------------------
