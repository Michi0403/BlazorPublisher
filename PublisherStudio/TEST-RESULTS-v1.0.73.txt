The v1.0.73 project-import suite verifies:

  reference, explicit placement/rate/speed, gap, and adapter-metadata model;
- OTIO, OTIOZ, MLT/Kdenlive/Shotcut, XGES, OpenShot OSP, and CMX 3600 EDL
  adapter entry points;
- bounded JSON/XML/archive processing, prohibited XML DTD/external resolution,

  network-fetch APIs;
- Video Studio ribbon/context import and relink commands, active-track
  projection, compatibility reporting, drag/drop routing, publication storage,
  and version/format alignment;
- the documented distinction between project interchange, media decoding, and
  Mainframe-oriented OBS scene import.

or alter the test result.
