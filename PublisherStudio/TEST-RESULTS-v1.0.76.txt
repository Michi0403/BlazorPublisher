Executable playback cancellation test
-------------------------------------
- A fake HTMLMediaElement starts a pending play() request.
- pauseMedia() interrupts it with an AbortError carrying the browser message
  "The play() request was interrupted by a call to pause()."
- playMediaRange() resolves false instead of rejecting.
- This verifies the expected cancellation cannot escape into JSInterop or
  Blazor Server RemoteRenderer.
